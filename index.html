DEEPWICK

Survive the night. Dig deep. Bring the light home.

Mouse + keyboard or controller · progress saves automatically

You wake beside the cold Wick.

For a hundred years the great lantern of Deepwick kept the dark inside the mountain. Then something far below began drinking the light. The Wick went cold, the town emptied, and now when the sun sets the Hollows climb out to look for more.

Punch a tree, dig for coal, and kindle the Wick before the first night falls: nothing from the dark survives in its light. Then go down, and carry light. The dark wears on your nerve, and the things that live in it come for a delver who has lost theirs. Everything that glows down there is light you can bring home to feed the flame.

As the Wick grows, the townsfolk come back. And somewhere under five layers of rock, the Gloamheart is beating.

DEEPWICK

Survive the night. Dig deep. Bring the light home.

Mouse + keyboard · progress saves automatically

You wake in the meadow with nothing.

No pick, no lantern, no food. Just a valley of trees, a mountain full of tunnels, and the old mining town of Deepwick long gone quiet. Something far below has started drinking the light, and when the sun sets the Hollows climb out to look for more.

Punch a tree. Build a workbench. Make a pick, a shelter, a door and a bed before the first night falls. Then go down: smelt ore, forge better tools, find the heart crystals and relics buried in the dark.

Build deeds and settlers will come: Pell to trade, Brannoc to sharpen your steel, Wren to read the relics. And somewhere under five layers of rock, the Gloamheart is beating.

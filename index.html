Dig deep. Keep your lantern lit. Bring the light home.

Mouse + keyboard · progress saves automatically

The lanterns are going out.

For a hundred years the mining town of Deepwick has lived on what the mountain gives: coal, copper, silver, the odd ruby. But something far below has started drinking the light. Lanterns gutter. Tunnels go cold. The old lift stations fell silent one by one.

Pell will buy what you dig up. Brannoc will turn it into better picks, brighter lanterns and boots that bounce. Wren wants every relic you can find and has a board full of bounties.

Somewhere under five layers of rock, the Gloamheart is beating. Grind your gear, restore the lifts, and go stop it.
